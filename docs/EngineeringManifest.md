# Engineering Manifest

## Think First

Before writing code,
understand the problem.

Before designing,
understand the business.

Before optimizing,
measure first.

Before creating abstraction,
prove the need.

Before adding dependency,
evaluate necessity.

Before creating module,
question the responsibility.

Before changing architecture,
write ADR.

---

## Build with Purpose

Code exists to serve learning.

Architecture serves implementation.

Learning habit is the product.

Knowledge is the asset.

AI is replaceable.

Domain is forever.

Offline is the default.

Deterministic over magic.

Simple over clever.

Maintainable over complex.

---

## Engineering Discipline

Every business rule belongs to the Domain.

UI never owns business logic.

Infrastructure never dictates business rules.

Measure before optimizing.

Test before merging.

Benchmark before releasing.

Document decisions, not assumptions.

Evolve through ADR, not intuition.

---

## Team Mindset

Build for years, not for demos.

Prefer clarity over cleverness.

Optimize only when necessary.

Leave the codebase better than you found it.

Every commit should move the product forward.
